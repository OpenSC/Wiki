# Italian Infocamere

This page has been merged into **[Italian CNS, CIE and digital signature](Italian-CNS-and-CIE)**.

Infocamere is the IT company of the Italian Chambers of Commerce, which issue CNS cards to company
representatives. OpenSC 0.10.0 and later supports types 1202, 1203, 1400 and 1600 of the Infocamere
card; the `itacns` PKCS#15 emulation also knows the Infocamere 1204 certificate layout. See
[Italian CNS, CIE and digital signature](Italian-CNS-and-CIE) for the current state of support.
